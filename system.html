A small system slice

The tokens and components behind the first-ticket prototype. It keeps VIP Monkey's mint, near-black, Montserrat and monkey, and gives them clear roles. Everything here is a proposal, not VIP Monkey's current system. Values live in tokens.css; this page uses the prototype's own CSS, so it can't drift from the screens.

Colour roles

Mint does two jobs only: the brand surface and the primary action, always with black text. Indicators on white become near-black (today's mint tab underline and slider are 1.55:1 and 1.35:1). Amber and red are kept for real problems; the checkout hold is neutral until the last two minutes.

Type

Montserrat carries the brand in titles, prices and buttons. Body text, labels and lists use the iOS system face (San Francisco), which Apple recommends for reading and which scales with the phone's text-size setting. Sizes follow iOS Dynamic Type defaults; nothing is below 11 pt.

Inputs and choices

Labels sit above fields, never inside them. Errors say what is wrong and what to do. Organiser questions get real controls: Ja / Nej, a switch for "not in school", date as three native pickers (iOS shows its wheel), so nobody types YYYYMMDD.

Not in this slice

  • Dark mode for the whole app. The door ticket already uses a dark surface; a full dark theme is the next step.
  • Apple Pay. VIP Monkey offers Swish and card today; Apple Pay would remove the card step on iPhone and is worth testing if their payment provider supports it.
  • Android. The review evidence says Android is where the app fails most, so the same components would need a Material pass.
